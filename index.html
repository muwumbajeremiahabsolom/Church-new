Who we are

A church built by neighbors, for neighbors.

GSMChurch started around a folding table in a living room, and it still feels that way, just with better chairs. We're a mixed congregation of longtime locals, new arrivals, young families, and people who haven't set foot in a church in years.

We don't ask you to check your questions at the door. We'd rather sit with them together, over scripture, over shared meals, and over the long work of showing up for one another.

What to expect on Sunday

Arrive

Come as you are

Jeans and boots are as welcome as Sunday best. Doors open thirty minutes early for coffee and conversation.

Kids

Cared for, downstairs

Nursery through 5th grade have their own space and their own lesson, so you can worship without watching the clock.

After

Stay for the good part

Every service ends in the fellowship hall with real coffee and real conversation. No pressure to linger or to leave.

Current sermon

So Great a Salvation: Pay Attention, Don't Drift

The writer of Hebrews warns that the greatest danger isn't open rebellion, it's slow drift. This message looks at why we ought to give earnest heed to what we've heard, and what God has done to confirm it. New messages are posted every Monday.
